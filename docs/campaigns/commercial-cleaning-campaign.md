# Commercial Cleaning Industry Campaign — CARSI LMS

## GP-137 | Priority: P0 Urgent

## Objective

Full catalogue push to Australia's cleaning businesses.
Target: 1,000 enrolled cleaning professionals in 12 months.

## Target Audience

- Owner-operators of commercial cleaning companies (1-20 staff)
- Franchise managers (Jani-King, Jim's Cleaning, Coverall)
- Facility managers procuring cleaning services
- Individual cleaners seeking career advancement

## Key Value Propositions

1. **Win Tenders** — Some government and corporate tenders list IICRC certification, which comes from IICRC-approved schools; CARSI adds documented training
2. **Wider Services** — Restoration skills let cleaners quote technical work
3. **Infection Control** — Post-COVID, clients demand verifiable hygiene credentials
4. **Quick Turnaround** — Most modules completable in 2-4 hours; no lost workdays
