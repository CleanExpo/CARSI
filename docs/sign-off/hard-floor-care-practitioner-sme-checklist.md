**For:** the subject-matter expert (SME) reviewing this draft. **Ledger:** `data/courses/hard-floor-care/v1-draft/evidence/evidence-ledger.jsonl` with 108 claims: 44 VERIFIED, 38 JUSTIFIED, **26 GAP (listed below)**.

Only the GAP claims, the open items and the JUSTIFIED claims that name an SME check (all listed below) need an answer. VERIFIED claims carry a source, and the other JUSTIFIED claims carry stated reasoning. Tick one box per claim:

- **OK**: the claim is correct as worded.
- **Fix**: write the correct wording beside it.
- **Cut**: remove the claim from the course.

## Asbestos hold (read first)

Asbestos is held. The course teaches identify, presume, stop and refer, and it gives no work method for any floor that may contain asbestos. Asbestos cannot be ruled out by looking. A floor that cannot be ruled out in writing is presumed to contain it, and work stops. A qualified reviewer must release this hold, and the course must not be seeded or published until they do. The practical day uses documented panels and boards only, and no task involves suspected asbestos-containing material.

## Other holds

- No universal pressure, pH band, pad colour or machine speed is stated as a rule. A specific figure appears only as an attributed example from a source that was opened, such as one flooring maker's guide.
- No standard text was read or reproduced. No certification or continuing-education credit is claimed.
- The course is a draft. status is draft, isPublished is false, cecHours is 0 and iicrcDiscipline is null.
