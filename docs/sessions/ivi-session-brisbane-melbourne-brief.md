  (continuing-education credit for technicians maintaining an existing IICRC certification).

  and better documentation on the job.

> **Say it precisely (licence-critical):** CARSI is **"IICRC CEC Accredited"** and courses **"earn
> IICRC CECs."** Never say CARSI delivers "IICRC courses", is "IICRC accredited", or that you can
> "get IICRC certified with CARSI." CARSI issues **CARSI designations**; IICRC certification is
> obtained only through IICRC-approved schools — CARSI courses earn CECs toward *maintaining* it.

**The hook for `[IVI]`:** most training teaches technique and stops. CARSI closes the loop —
**learn the principle → apply it on the job with RestoreAssist → document the outcome → improve the
next job** — and now wants to add the **business** layer (running a profitable, well-run firm), which
is where a Business Coaching course comes in.

  **yours to decide** — I've left them open.)

### B. The RestoreAssist / field tie-in (the "AI integration")
- CARSI = the learning + credential layer; **RestoreAssist = the field decision-support layer.**
  Together: learn → apply → document → improve.
- Careful language: RestoreAssist **supports** judgement and documentation; it **does not replace**
  standards, SDS/TDS, or a qualified professional. (No overclaiming — same rule as the courses.)
- For a Business Coaching course this is the differentiator: coaching that plugs into the tools the
  firm already uses on site, not generic business advice.

### C. Why CARSI is credible (proof, not adjectives)
- 54 live courses; 38 IICRC CEC Accredited; a real catalogue and LMS the learner can use today.
- Australian-produced throughout (AU English, 230 V / AS·NZS, metric, AUD) — built for this market.
- Business model: annual **membership (~$795)** + course sales + team seats; the directory/registry
  and benchmark give long-term authority.
