Use ChatGPT for one useful business task

This guide uses ChatGPT by OpenAI. Follow one task from beginning to end. All business information below is fictional.

Follow the recorded example first: the exact café facts, prompt and correction match Margot’s demonstration. The six examples below are separate practice exercises; their wording and word limits may differ from the recording.

Before you send

  • Don’t press Enter yet. Finish the facts and instruction first.
  • Shift + Enter adds a new line in the desktop browser composer shown in the recording. Your message stays unsent.
  • Enter or the Send arrow submits the completed message to ChatGPT. Read the whole prompt before sending. This does not send a customer email.
  • Keep corrections in the same chat so the earlier facts and response remain in context.

These keyboard instructions refer to the recorded desktop browser session. Controls may differ in other interfaces.

Start here

  1. Open chatgpt.com in your browser and sign in to your existing approved account.
  2. Start a new chat. Use fictional inputs and your organisation’s approved account settings. Exact menu labels can vary; this guide does not require a particular temporary-chat label.
  3. Choose one example below. Copy its facts and prompt together into the message box at the bottom of the chat. These examples use pasted text; no file upload or business connection is needed.
  4. Finish the complete prompt. Use Shift + Enter if you need a new line, then read it before selecting Send. Wait for the response and compare it with the checks provided.
  5. Ask for a correction in the same chat. Check the revised answer again.
  6. Use the draft only after your own approval. Sending a ChatGPT message does not send the draft to a customer.

Use fictional information for practice. Before using real business records, follow your organisation’s approved account and information-handling rules. If the account reaches a usage limit, retain the example and continue later; this guide requires no purchase.

What is a prompt? The instruction you type into the message box. What is the output? The draft or summary returned in the chat. It still needs checking.

1. Reply to a customer enquiry

New chat → paste facts → paste prompt → Send → check → revise → approve

A. Paste these fictional facts into the message box

FICTIONAL ENQUIRY: Can you supply 20 lunch boxes?
APPROVED FACTS: AUD $18 per box. Delivery fee AUD $25.
UNKNOWN: Date, delivery address, dietary requirements, capacity and GST treatment.
No booking has been accepted.

B. Add this instruction beneath the facts, then select Send

Use only the supplied facts. Draft a professional reply under 120 words. Show the food amount and delivery separately. Ask for the missing information. Do not add GST, promise availability, accept a booking or send anything.

C. Compare the response with the source

20 × $18 = $360; $360 + $25 = $385 before any GST adjustment. The draft must not call this a final GST-inclusive quote, invent a date or claim an allergy can be accommodated.

Prepared example of a suitable draft

Thanks for your enquiry about 20 lunch boxes. At AUD $18 each, the food amount is $360, plus $25 delivery: $385 before any GST adjustment. Please confirm the date, delivery address and dietary requirements. We will check availability and whether we can meet those requirements, and confirm the GST treatment before issuing a final quote. No booking is confirmed.

This is an authored teaching example, not a captured ChatGPT response. The wording you receive may differ.

D. If needed, send this follow-up in the same chat

Revise the reply to state that availability and dietary requirements need confirmation. Label $385 as the amount before any GST adjustment, subject to confirming GST treatment.

E. Finish the task yourself

A draft reply and a short list of questions. Copy it into your email only after checking and approving it.

2. Prepare an estimate

New chat → paste facts → paste prompt → Send → check → revise → approve

A. Paste these fictional facts into the message box

FICTIONAL JOB: 3 hours of general maintenance.
APPROVED RATES: AUD $80 per hour; materials allowance AUD $45.
UNKNOWN: Scope confirmation, access, timing and GST treatment.

B. Add this instruction beneath the facts, then select Send

Prepare an internal estimate using only these facts. Show labour, materials and arithmetic. List assumptions and unanswered questions separately. Do not call it a final quote, add GST or promise a date.

C. Compare the response with the source

3 × $80 = $240; $240 + $45 = $285 before any GST adjustment. Check scope and rates against your source. Do not send the internal estimate as an approved quote.

D. If needed, send this follow-up in the same chat

Keep the amount provisional. Add a checklist of information I must confirm before issuing a quote.

E. Finish the task yourself

An internal estimate to review, then enter into your normal quoting process.

5. Summarise a week of sales

New chat → paste facts → paste prompt → Send → check → revise → approve

A. Paste these fictional facts into the message box

FICTIONAL SALES, AUD:
Monday,120
Tuesday,150
Wednesday,90
Thursday,missing
Friday,140

B. Add this instruction beneath the facts, then select Send

Summarise these daily sales. Show the total for supplied numeric values and the calculation. Identify missing days. Do not treat missing as zero or present a complete weekly total.

C. Compare the response with the source

$120 + $150 + $90 + $140 = $500 for the four supplied days. Thursday is missing. The complete five-day total cannot be calculated.

D. If needed, send this follow-up in the same chat

Rename the total to Known sales for four days. Add Thursday missing immediately below it.

E. Finish the task yourself

A provisional summary to compare with the original sales records. Add the missing record before reporting a complete week.

6. Prepare a staff handover

New chat → paste facts → paste prompt → Send → check → revise → approve

A. Paste these fictional facts into the message box

FICTIONAL SHOP NOTES: Till counted; amount recorded in the approved till sheet. Order SAMPLE-104 packed but not collected. Rear light reported faulty; supervisor informed. Saturday roster awaiting approval.

B. Add this instruction beneath the facts, then select Send

Write a staff handover with Completed, Outstanding and Needs approval sections. Use only these notes. Do not invent till amounts, mark the order collected, declare the light safe or approve the roster.

C. Compare the response with the source

Packed does not mean collected. A fault report is not a repair or a safety clearance. The roster is still awaiting approval. Till amount is not provided here.

D. If needed, send this follow-up in the same chat

Add a named-owner field marked To assign for each outstanding action. Keep safety decisions with the supervisor.

E. Finish the task yourself

A handover draft for the outgoing staff member to check and pass through the normal process.

Test five cases before adopting the workflow

Margot asks you to test five samples. Use the café workflow with these fictional variations, one case per new chat. These are authored practice cases, not additional recorded ChatGPT runs.

  1. Known quantities: 20 lunch boxes at A$18 each, plus A$25 delivery. GST, date, address, availability and allergy requirements remain unknown. Check that the estimate is A$385 and missing facts stay visible.
  2. Missing price: 20 lunch boxes; delivery A$25; box price not supplied. Check that the draft asks for the price and gives no invented total.
  3. Contradictory notes: One note says 20 lunch boxes, another says 24; price A$18 each, delivery A$25. Check that the draft asks which quantity is correct instead of choosing one.
  4. Unconfirmed date: The customer asks for “next Tuesday”; no exact date, address or availability is confirmed. Check that the draft asks for confirmation and makes no booking promise.
  5. Correction: Begin with 20 lunch boxes at A$18 plus A$25 delivery. After the first answer, state in the same chat: “Correction: the quantity is 18, not 20. All unknowns remain unknown. Revise the estimate and draft.” Check 18 × A$18 + A$25 = A$349, then check the whole revised answer.

For each case, record: case name; time spent preparing and checking; corrections needed; whether every fact, amount and promise passes your check. Use your own normal process as the comparison. Five samples are a practice starting point, not proof of general reliability or a guaranteed saving.
