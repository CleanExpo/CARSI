# Follow the demonstration

Use a clean temporary ChatGPT session and fictional information.

1. Click the message box and type these facts:

Fictional cafe enquiry: 20 lunch boxes at A$18 each; delivery A$25. Customer asks for next Tuesday. GST treatment, exact date, delivery address, availability and allergy requirements are unknown.

2. **Do not press Enter yet.** Use **Shift + Enter** to add a new line without sending. Add this request below the facts:

Draft a short customer email of no more than 90 words. Show 20 x A$18 + A$25 = A$385. Ask for missing details. Do not invent, add GST, confirm the order or promise allergy suitability. Give only the email draft, without commentary.

3. Read the complete prompt, then click the ChatGPT Send arrow.

4. Read the full answer. Verify A$385 independently. The captured first answer inferred a date; the enquiry has not confirmed it.

5. Type this correction in the same chat:

Rewrite as plain text in the chat, not the email composer. Keep under 65 words. Ask the customer to confirm the exact date and delivery address; do not infer a date. Keep A$385 as an estimate, leave GST unresolved, confirm availability later and do not promise allergy suitability.

6. Read it, click the ChatGPT Send arrow, and check the revised answer. It remains a draft requiring human approval. The inline email composer is part of the observed OpenAI response; no email is sent.


## Keyboard tips shown in this revision

- **Do not press Enter yet:** finish the facts and the request first.
- **Shift + Enter:** add a new line inside the message. It does not send the prompt. The video demonstrates the actual new paragraph in ChatGPT.
- **Enter, or the Send arrow:** submit only after checking the complete prompt. Sending a prompt to ChatGPT does not send a customer email.
- **Keep corrections in the same chat:** the earlier facts and answer remain available as context.

These tips describe the desktop browser demonstration captured on 09/11/2026.
