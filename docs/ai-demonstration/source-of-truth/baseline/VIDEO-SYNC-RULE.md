# Required tutorial video synchronisation contract — 08/11/2026

Founder authorised after repeated stalls and narration/action mismatch in MAC-MELBOURNE-AI-BUSINESS. Applies to future narrated software demonstrations and repairs.

1. One frame-based timeline owns actual narration clips, visible actions, captions and completed-text reading windows. Measure audio after synthesis. Derive all tracks from the same manifest; do not independently estimate their times.
2. Capture clean fictional sessions at a constant frame rate with continuous video and action timestamps. Detect missing motion and batched updates before writing narration. Keep clean source recordings. A sparse screenshot sequence cannot be represented as continuous recorded keystrokes. Recapture defective motion; do not keep applying offset patches to it.
3. Anchor each spoken instruction to the visible corresponding event, including actual spoken-word onset for clicks. Target at most 250 ms mismatch. Have a reviewer check the final artifact at each anchor; audio correlation alone cannot prove semantic synchronisation.
4. During active typing, any unexplained unchanged text interval over 750 ms requires repair or explicit source qualification. Reading holds are separate timeline events: show the complete text, allow at least 3 seconds and budget at 180 words/minute unless the owner approves another pace. Do not silently turn a captured freeze into a reading hold.
5. Run python3 ~/.unite-group/video-sync/verify_sync.py MANIFEST before claiming production readiness. It fails closed for missing or mismatched evidence, action drift, unexplained stalls, speech overlap, insufficient reading time or incomplete playback. A PASS checks declared measurements and evidence integrity; it does not independently understand the pictures or replace an independent watch/listen or owner acceptance.
6. Review the exact final MP4, identified by SHA256, naturally from beginning to end at 1x with sound enabled. Save playback events, action checkpoint frames, every-second privacy review and measured audio. No waiting/stalled/error events or unexplained resets. Fullscreen must preserve current position; replay is explicit.
7. Missing evidence = HOLD. Failed owner review = HOLD regardless of technical checks. No publication, paid regeneration, release or acceptance authority is granted here. Fix locally within current authorisation; recapture/rebuild rather than another unchanged repair loop. Existing reliability counters and release laws remain binding.
8. When the original plays smoothly and the export hesitates, rebuild directly from the original media. Preserve complete spoken sentences and the original avatar cadence; never manufacture a sentence with a borrowed word or spread its phrases across reading holds. Do not inherit audio or avatar footage from an already edited export. Record the delivery codec, range, frame cadence and packet timestamps, compare source audio placement throughout the exported passages, and review the exact export before replaying it for the owner. Codec differences alone are a hypothesis, not proof of the playback cause.

This is a local production workflow rule, not a modification to any AI model. Integration with every remote pipeline is not implied by installation on this machine.
