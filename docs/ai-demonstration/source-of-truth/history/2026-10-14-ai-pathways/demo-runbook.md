# Demo runbook: a Project that knows the business

Chosen demo: a Claude Project for a made-up business ("Example Cleaning Co"). Slot: 7:00 to 10:00 (3:00). Why this one: it works for beginners and for 12-month users, needs no accounting login, and puts no real customer data on screen.
Facts used: Projects are Free up to 5, paid unlimited (https://claude.com/pricing). Memory has a setting that keeps sensitive topics out (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes). Re-check both on the day.

## Prep (day before)
1. Make the Project "Example Cleaning Co". No real names, addresses or photos anywhere.
2. Write the instructions (about 10 lines): who we are, services, quote in Australian dollars, GST shown separately, list exclusions, never promise a date, plain friendly tone.
3. Upload three files you wrote for this demo: `price-list.pdf` (current prices only), `sample-jobs.pdf` (3 invented jobs with scope and price), `how-we-write.pdf` (one invented customer email we sent).
4. Write the test enquiry in a text file: a burst pipe, a flooded laundry, a landlord wanting a quote this afternoon. Invented name and suburb.
5. Run it end to end once. Note the time it takes and save the draft quote.
6. Check Account > Privacy: see what the sensitive-topic and training settings show, so you can show that screen without surprises. Check memory contains nothing private.
7. Close other chats and tabs; turn off notifications and mail pop-ups. Sidebar must show no real client names.
8. Record the backup clip and screenshots (see below). Copy both to the laptop desktop and to a USB stick.

## Live steps (3:00)
| Time | Do | Say (see script) |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | Open the Project, show instructions | Who we are, our rules, written like a briefing for a new hire |
| 7:30 | Show the three files | Price list, sample jobs, how we write |
| 8:00 | Paste the enquiry, send | A new enquiry arrives |
| 8:15 | While it works, talk | I haven't explained prices or tone |
| 8:45 | Read the draft; point at each price | I check numbers against the price list |
| 9:15 | Type: "What assumptions did you make?" | That's where mistakes hide |
| 9:40 | Open the privacy settings screen | Find these before you use real work |
| 10:00 | Back to slides | |

## Failure triggers and switches
- No answer after 20 seconds, or an error: say "let me show you the one I made earlier" and play the clip. Do not retry more than once.
- Wrong or odd output: leave it in. Use it: "this is why a person checks". Then continue.
- Wifi down: switch the laptop to phone hotspot (test before the session). If still down, go straight to the clip.
- Projector or screen-share fault: use the screenshots in the printed handout pack and talk through them.

## Backup C: no screen at all
Talk through the handout ladder row for L2 and take two questions from the room. Skip to the safety section on time.

## After
Delete the demo Project's invented data if you reuse the account for real work. Keep the clip for next time.
