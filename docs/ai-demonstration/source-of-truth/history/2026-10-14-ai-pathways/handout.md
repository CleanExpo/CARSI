# AI Pathways for small business (one page, A4)
Phill McGurk, Melbourne. Facts read 14 Oct 2026. Not legal or financial advice.

## The ladder
| Rung | Exercise (15 min) | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| L0 Chat | Paste a customer email with the name and contact details removed. Ask for a reply in your voice, then "what did I miss?" | Treating answers as facts |
| L1 Documents | Upload a price list and a job note. Ask for a quote table, then "question my assumptions". | Prompt tourism: new tricks, no repeatable result |
| L2 Knows you | Build a Project: services, pricing rules, tone, 3 sample jobs. Test on a new enquiry. | Stale or clashing files; training left on |
| L3 Connected | Connect email read-only. "Unanswered emails older than 2 days: draft replies, don't send." | Wide permissions; unchecked numbers |
| L4 Delegates | Give an agent 10 job notes; ask for one weekly summary sheet. Stay on Manual approval. | Skip mode near money or customers |
| L5 Scheduled | Monday 7am: summarise last week's enquiries and overdue invoices; draft chasers, never send. | Unattended emails, no one reviewing |

## Find your rung: score each A=0, B=1, C=2, D=3
1. Weekly use: A never, B ask questions, C upload files and reuse instructions, D it runs tasks unasked
2. Project, custom instructions or memory: A no, B tried once, C one in use, D several, kept current
3. AI reads real email, calendar, Drive or accounts: A no, B pasted bits, C connected read-only, D connected with approvals
4. Multi-step job, only the result reviewed: A no, B once, C regularly, D with scheduling
5. Anything scheduled while you're away: A no, B tested one, C one or two live, D several, reviewed weekly
6. Know if chats train the model, per tool: A no idea, B partly, C checked and set, D checked and written down
7. If an AI error reached a customer: A unsure, B me, no process, C me, spot checks, D named owner and review log
8. Which app you pay for: A none, B one, by habit, C chosen for a workflow, D chosen per task and costed

Score 0 to 24: 0-3 = L0; 4-8 = L1; 9-12 = L2; 13-16 = L3; 17-20 = L4; 21-24 = L5.
Override: if Q3, Q4 and Q5 are all A or B, you are at the chatbot stage whatever the total. Start at L2 and L3.

## Safety and the one-page policy
OAIC: do not put personal information, sensitive information especially, into publicly available AI tools. Check each tool's training setting. National AI Centre's six practices, one line each: accountability (name an owner), impact planning, risk management (what never goes in), information sharing, testing and monitoring (monthly output review), human control (a person reads it first).
