## 9. What the OAIC says (1:00)
- On screen: Privacy law applies to what you paste in
- On screen: Keep personal information out of public AI tools
- Visual: a customer job file with the names and photos blurred, a red line through the arrow into the AI app.
- Notes: The Privacy Act and the Australian Privacy Principles apply to personal information put into AI and to AI output that contains it; do due diligence; update your privacy policy; made-up personal information counts; OAIC recommends not entering personal information, especially sensitive information, into publicly available generative AI tools. https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/guidance-on-privacy-and-the-use-of-commercially-available-ai-products
- Notes: [HUMAN INPUT NEEDED: Phill story about a privacy mistake or near miss with AI]
- Notes: Not legal advice; the audience should check whether the Privacy Act covers their business.

## 10. Check the training switch on every tool (0:45)
- On screen: Each app has its own rule
- On screen: Claude personal: opt-in. Gemini: Keep Activity.
- Visual: two toggle switches drawn side by side with different labels.
- Notes: Claude consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max) use chats for model improvement only if you opt in via privacy settings, or if flagged for safety; incognito chats are never used. https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training
- Notes: Gemini chats may train models when Keep Activity is on; with it off, or in Temporary Chat, not used for training and kept 72 hours. https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961
