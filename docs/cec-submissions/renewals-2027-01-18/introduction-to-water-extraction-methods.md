## 4. Course summary and learning objectives

**Summary.** Water extraction is the most time-critical task in any water damage response. Every minute standing water remains in contact with porous building materials increases absorption depth, extends the drying timeline, and increases secondary damage costs. This course covers the operational fundamentals of professional water extraction: why it matters, how to do it safely, what equipment to use, how to apply the right technique for the material type, and how to manage waste correctly.

**Learning objectives / curriculum outline.**

- MODULE 1: Purpose of Extraction
- MODULE 2: Safety
- MODULE 3: Extraction Equipment
- MODULE 4: Extraction Techniques
- MODULE 5: Waste Disposal
- WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
- MODULE 6: Moisture Monitoring During and After Extraction
- MODULE 7: Extraction on Different Floor Systems
- What you learnt
