## 4. Course summary and learning objectives

**Summary.** Water damage restoration creates a unique combination of overlapping hazards — electrical, biological, chemical, physical, and ergonomic — that can escalate rapidly if not managed with structured safety procedures. This course provides technicians with the practical protocols to assess hazards, select PPE, control exposures, and manage equipment safely in every water damage scenario.

**Learning objectives / curriculum outline.**

- Module 1: Assessing Hazards
- Module 2: Use of PPE
- Module 3: Exposure Control
- Module 4: Equipment Safety
- Module 5: Working Safely Around Water and Electricity
- Module 6: Communicating Hazards on Site
- Module 7: Emergency Response and Incident Reporting
- What you learnt
