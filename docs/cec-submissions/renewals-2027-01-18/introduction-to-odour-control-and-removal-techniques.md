## 4. Course summary and learning objectives

**Summary.** This course provides restoration and cleaning professionals with the practical knowledge to identify, assess, contain, and eliminate odour in residential and commercial environments. Covering odour chemistry, containment strategies, air purification, counteractants, and surface treatment methods, the course is grounded in real-world application and aligned with Australian workplace safety requirements. The focus throughout is on source elimination, not odour masking.

**Learning objectives / curriculum outline.**

- Module 1: Odour Basics
- Module 2: Containment Strategies
- Module 3: Air Purification Methods
- Module 4: Odour Counteractants
- Module 5: Surface Treatments
- SECTION 2: SMOKE AND SOOT DAMAGE COURSES
- Module 6: Odour Documentation and Clearance
- Module 7: Special Odour Scenarios
- What you learnt
