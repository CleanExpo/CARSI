## 4. Course summary and learning objectives

**Summary.** Introduction to Basic Carpet Cleaning and Drying is a foundational course developed by CARSI — the Centre for Applied Restoration and Specialist Industries. This course is designed specifically for technicians entering the carpet cleaning and restoration industry in Australia, and for those already working in the field who wish to consolidate and formalise their practical knowledge. It also incorporates guidance from Safe Work Australia, relevant Australian Standards, and accepted best practice across the restoration industry in Australia.

**Learning objectives / curriculum outline.**

- Carpet Construction
- Water Damage Effects
- Cleaning Equipment
- Cleaning Products
- Drying Techniques
- Pre-Inspection and Job Setup
- Carpet Fibre Identification
- What you learnt
