**Subject:** CEC course approval — 10 online courses, Cleaning and Restoration Science Institute (CARSI), Australia

Hello,

I am writing to submit ten online courses for IICRC Continuing Education Credit approval.

CARSI (Cleaning and Restoration Science Institute) is an Australian provider of continuing
education for cleaning, restoration and indoor-air-quality technicians, listed in the IICRC CEC
Provider Directory. Our courses are written for Australian conditions — AS/NZS and Safe Work
Australia requirements, 230 V / 50 Hz equipment, metric units — and are taken largely by
technicians maintaining IICRC certifications they already hold.

Each course is delivered online and self-paced, with an assessment and a certificate of
completion recording the learner, the course and the hours. A submission pack is attached for
each one, covering provider details, title, delivery and duration, summary and learning
objectives, instructor, point of contact and supporting documentation.
