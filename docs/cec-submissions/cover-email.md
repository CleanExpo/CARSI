**Subject:** CEC course approval — 10 online courses, CARSI (Australia)

---

Dear CEC Committee,

I am writing to submit ten online courses for IICRC CEC approval.

CARSI is an Australian online continuing education provider for the cleaning and restoration
trade. We are an IICRC CEC Accredited provider. Our courses are self-paced and delivered online at
https://www.carsi.com.au, which is how Australian technicians keep their certifications current
without the flights, hotels and lost days that classroom delivery costs them here.

The ten courses in this submission are our restoration and indoor-air-quality titles, ranging from
1.0 to 2.0 educational hours. Each attached pack follows the information your process asks for:
provider name and website, course title, delivery dates and duration, summary and learning
objectives, instructor, point of contact, and the supporting documentation available.

Two things I want to be explicit about, because I would rather raise them than have you find them.

First, on how we describe ourselves. CARSI issues its own credentials — the CARSI Southern
Hemisphere Restoration Designations. We do not deliver IICRC certification and we do not describe
our courses as IICRC courses. Every course page on our site states that a CARSI course gives a
CARSI credential, not an IICRC certification, and that IICRC certification is obtained through an
IICRC-approved school and examination. If any wording in these packs reads otherwise, tell me and
I will correct it.

Second, on CEC hours. We display none today. Our approvals register is empty by design: a course
shows CEC hours only after your committee has approved that specific course, and until then our
platform shows nothing. This submission is the first step in changing that, and I would rather
have zero approved courses displaying honestly than an inferred number displaying wrongly.

I am happy to supply anything further — full course content, assessment instruments, instructor
credentials, or a reviewer account on the platform so you can work through a course as a student
would.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,

Phill McGurk
Founder, CARSI Pty Ltd
support@carsi.com.au · https://www.carsi.com.au
