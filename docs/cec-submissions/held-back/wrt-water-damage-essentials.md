## 4. Course summary and learning objectives

**Summary.** Introduces the fundamentals of water damage restoration: categories of water, classes of loss, safety, and the core drying workflow.

**Learning objectives / curriculum outline.**

- Module — Water Damage Fundamentals: Categories and Classes of Water Damage — Reading
- Module — Module 1 — Safety, standards & site control: Orientation and safe work on loss sites
- Module — Module 2 — Water categories & material science: Understanding contamination and porous assemblies
- Module — Module 3 — Initial inspection & moisture mapping: Systematic inspection and baseline readings
- Module — Module 4 — Water extraction principles: Removing liquid water efficiently
- Module — Module 5 — Evaporation & dehumidification: Balancing airflow and grain depression
- Module — Module 6 — Equipment selection & placement: Air movers, LGRs, and heat — when and why
- Module — Module 7 — Monitoring & psychrometry basics: Reading conditions and adjusting the plan
- Module — Module 8 — Completion, handover & reporting: Defensible close-out
