## 1. The Topic

**Headline topic (both cities, both formats):**

> **"The job doesn't end when you pack up."** — how good training, the right gear, and clean
> documentation turn every clean and restoration job into evidence you can defend, bill and grow on.

- **Tradeshow floor theme:** *See it, try it, document it* — training, equipment and RestoreAssist
  in one room.
- **Trade Night talk (Phill McGurk):** *"Decisions and the paper trail: what separates a one-off
  job from a business."*

**Why this topic works for a mixed room:** cleaners and restorers do different work, but both win or
lose on the same three things — making the right call on site, backing it with the right equipment,
and leaving a record the customer, the builder or the insurer trusts. That is the loop CARSI closes:
**learn it → apply it on the job with RestoreAssist → document it → win the next job.** RestoreAssist
is the natural demo because it is where training meets the real job for both audiences.

## 2. Discussion points

### A. Tradeshow (daytime floor)

Stations and the conversation at each:

1. **RestoreAssist demo bench (the hero).** Turn site notes, photos and readings into a clear
   decision and a clean record — a drying/moisture story for restorers, a scope-and-condition
   record for cleaners. *Every visitor leaves having booked a 1:1 demo.*
2. **CARSI training wall.** Over 50 courses live; the CARSI Southern Hemisphere Restoration
   Designations (Water, Mould, Fire & Smoke, Structural Drying, Contaminated-Water, Trauma/Biohazard,
   Carpet, Timber Floor, Indoor-Environment); and that CARSI is an **IICRC CEC Accredited provider**
   with **38 courses approved for IICRC CECs** (credit toward maintaining an existing IICRC cert).
3. **CCW equipment & chemistry.** The gear and products behind the decisions — compare, ask, and
   connect what you buy to how the job is done and documented.
4. **"What's your next job?" clinic.** Short, practical talk-throughs: category/class and a dry
   goal, a safe-work and PPE call, a quoting-with-confidence conversation.

### B. Trade Night (evening — Phill McGurk talk + networking)

Run sheet:

1. **Welcome (5 min)** — host frames the night: one idea, then time to talk.
2. **Phill McGurk talk (20–25 min)** — *"Decisions and the paper trail."* Talking points:
   - The job is a chain of decisions; the money leaks at the decisions you can't back up later.
   - Three decisions that make or break a job (safety/scope, the right method & gear, the dry/clean
     goal) — one cleaning example, one restoration example.
   - Why documentation is not paperwork: it is how you get paid, defend the job and earn the next
     referral — especially with builders, strata and insurers.
   - Where training and the tool fit: learn the principle, apply it on the job, let RestoreAssist
     carry the record so it is done once and done right.
   - Honest limits: the tool and the training support your judgement; they do not replace a
     standard, an SDS or a qualified professional.
3. **Q&A (10 min).**
4. **Networking + stands open (45–60 min)** — RestoreAssist demo bench and CCW gear stay open; the
   one ask of the night is *book your demo*.
