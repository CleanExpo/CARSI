# Clearance and job-file checklist

**CARSI Contaminated-Water Practitioner — field tool (Module 6)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian-produced for Australia and New Zealand: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, RCD, metric units, AUD.*

## Order of work

Contain → Remove → Clean and disinfect → **Verify clean** → Dry → **Verify dry** → Hand over.

**Mould found?** AFDs and dehumidifiers only. No air movers across mouldy surfaces until cleaned and cleared.

## Verify clean

- [ ] All contaminated porous material removed
- [ ] Every remaining surface visibly clean, no soil, film or residue
- [ ] Cleaning and disinfection recorded (product, dilution, contact time)
- [ ] Containment intact until the check is done
- [ ] Photographs of each area, dated

## Verify dry

- [ ] Same points, same meter, at or below the dry standard, stable — dates: ____ / ____ / ____

## The job file (eight records)

- [ ] 1 Arrival: source, category and why, photos
- [ ] 2 Risk assessment or SWMS, inductions
- [ ] 3 PPE worn, fit test dates
- [ ] 4 Removal log with areas and reasons
- [ ] 5 Products with approval, dilution, contact time
- [ ] 6 Wastewater record and waste dockets
- [ ] 7 Drying readings
- [ ] 8 Clearance and signed handover
