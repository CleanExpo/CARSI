# PPE and decontamination card

**CARSI Contaminated-Water Practitioner — field tool (Module 3)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian-produced for Australia and New Zealand: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, RCD, metric units, AUD.*

## PPE by exposure route (hands-on Category 3 work)

| Route | PPE | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breathing | P2 or higher respirator (AS/NZS 1716) | Fit-tested to the wearer; fit-checked every time it goes on |
| Eyes and face | Goggles or face shield | Splash-proof |
| Skin and body | Fluid-resistant coverall, hood up | No shorts, no bare arms |
| Hands | Inner glove + chemical- and fluid-resistant outer glove | Change outer gloves when torn or heavily soiled |
| Feet | Waterproof boots that can be cleaned, or overboots | |
| Cuts | Waterproof dressing before you start | |

**Fit testing:** Australia — before first use and after a facial change (SafeWork SA). New Zealand — at first issue, then at least yearly or after a significant facial change (WorkSafe NZ). **Beard or stubble?** Use a respirator that does not rely on a face seal, such as a hood-type powered respirator. A dust mask is not a fitted respirator.

## Three zones

DIRTY (work area) → TRANSITION (sheeting, bags, wash station) → CLEAN (rest of house, van). One way through. Nothing contaminated crosses unbagged.

## Removal order in the transition zone

- [ ] 1. Knock gross soil off boots and outer gloves
- [ ] 2. Outer gloves off, into the bag
- [ ] 3. Coverall off, rolled inside out
- [ ] 4. Boots or overboots off at the line
- [ ] 5. Goggles, then respirator, by the straps
- [ ] 6. Inner gloves off last
- [ ] 7. Wash hands and face with soap and water
- [ ] 8. Bag sealed, out the controlled route

**Never** eat, drink, smoke or vape in the work area or in work clothes. **Cut through a glove, splash to eyes or mouth?** Stop, decontaminate, wash, seek medical advice the same day, record it and tell your supervisor.
