# Category 3 risk assessment and SWMS prompts

**CARSI Contaminated-Water Practitioner — field tool (Module 2)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian-produced for Australia and New Zealand: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, RCD, metric units, AUD.*

## The six-point hazard walk (from the threshold)

| # | Check | Control (top of the hierarchy first) | Stop and escalate if… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Look: source, depth, who is inside | Do not wade in | Source still running and cannot be stopped |
| 2 | Power | Isolate at the board, confirm dead; RCD on every lead; leads tested and tagged (AS/NZS 3760) | Board or fittings under water: licensed electrician |
| 3 | Structure and slips | Keep out of the drop zone of wet ceilings; manage silt and leads | Sagging ceiling or soft floor: structural engineer |
| 4 | Asbestos | AU: assume if built or renovated before 31 December 2003 (national ban); most likely if before 1990. NZ: before 2000. Do not break it | Suspect material: licensed assessor |
| 5 | Confined spaces | Pits, sewers, tanks, tight subfloors: hydrogen sulphide, low oxygen | Entry needed: confined-space-trained crew only |
| 6 | People | Vulnerable occupants out; children and pets away | Cannot clear the area |

## Paperwork

- **Australia:** SWMS before high-risk construction work starts. Check the triggers: likely to disturb asbestos; in or near a confined space; near energised electrical installations; area that may have a contaminated or flammable atmosphere. Victoria: check WorkSafe Victoria requirements.
- **New Zealand:** record the same risk assessment in the format your PCBU uses; check WorkSafe New Zealand guidance for the task.
- [ ] Every worker inducted to the assessment and signed on.

## If a worker becomes seriously ill (for example, an infection from the work)

| Australia | New Zealand |
| --- | --- |
| Notify the WHS regulator immediately; preserve the site | Notify WorkSafe as soon as possible (0800 030 040); preserve the site |
| Infection significantly contributed to by work, including contact with human body substances (model WHS Regulations reg 699) | Serious infection significantly contributed to by work, including with micro-organisms or human bodily substances (HSWA s 23) |
| Keep the record | Keep the record at least five years |

**Primary duty:** the PCBU (AU and NZ; employer in Victoria). It cannot be handed to someone else.
