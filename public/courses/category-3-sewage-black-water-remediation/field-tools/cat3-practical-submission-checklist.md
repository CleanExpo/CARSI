# Practical submission checklist

**CARSI Contaminated-Water Practitioner — field tool (Module 6)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian-produced for Australia and New Zealand: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, RCD, metric units, AUD.*

Five criteria, 20 points each, pass at 80 points. Use these headings.

| Criterion | The assessor looks for | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Category call and loss size | Source, category and why; area in m²; volume in litres; who is kept out | [ ] |
| 2 Risk assessment and the law | Six-point hazard walk; controls top-down; 230 V isolation and RCD; asbestos assumption for the building age and country; SWMS (AU) or NZ record; notification steps | [ ] |
| 3 PPE and decontamination | PPE by route; fit test and fit check for the job's country; beard option; three zones; removal order | [ ] |
| 4 Containment, removal, cleaning and waste | Containment and HVAC; AFD m³/h sum; removal with areas and reasons; product record; wastewater route with approval or docket; solid-waste dockets | [ ] |
| 5 Drying, clearance and the file | Drying after verified cleaning; mould rule; 230 V budget per 10 A circuit; clearance evidence; plain-English handover; consumer guarantee; AUD invoice with GST | [ ] |

## Before you submit

- [ ] Every sum shows its units
- [ ] Every decision has a one-sentence reason
- [ ] Photos are captioned and dated
- [ ] Names and addresses removed from a real job
- [ ] Standards named only; no text copied from any standard
- [ ] Australian English and metric units throughout
- [ ] New Zealand job? NZ law, regulator and numbers used throughout

**Common resubmission reasons:** no reason for the category; PPE without a fit test; waste "taken away" with no route or docket; drying before cleaning was verified; power maths at the wrong voltage; uncaptioned photos.
