# Hazard-to-PPE selection card

**CARSI Water Restoration Practitioner — field tool (Module 3)**
*Australian-produced: 230 V / 10 A, AS/NZS, Safe Work Australia framing.*

## Step 1 — walk the hazards and control each (top of the hierarchy first)

| Hazard | First control |
| --- | --- |
| **Electrical** | Isolate 230 V supply at the board; confirm dead; RCD (safety switch) on portable gear; leads tested and tagged (AS/NZS 3760) |
| **Biological** | Category 2/3 water carries micro-organisms; sewage is a biohazard — contain and protect |
| **Structural** | Wet plaster ceilings/floors can collapse — do not work under a loaded ceiling; escalate to an engineer |
| **Chemical** | Read the SDS before using any product; ventilate |
| **Slip / trip** | Manage wet floors, trailing leads, ladders |
| **Confined space / heat** | Subfloors, roof voids, hot drying zones — assess before entry |

**Primary duty:** the PCBU must eliminate risks so far as reasonably practicable, or minimise them. **Eliminate > substitute / isolate / engineering > administrative > PPE.** PPE is the last line.

## Step 2 — select PPE by water category

- **Category 1 (clean):** safety footwear, gloves, eye protection (+ respirator if disturbing dusty/mouldy material).
- **Category 2 (grey):** above + fluid-resistant gloves + fit-tested **P2** respirator (fit-checked each wear) when generating aerosols.
- **Category 3 (black/sewage):** fluid-resistant coveralls, boots, gloves, eye protection + **P2 or higher** fit-tested respirator (fit-checked each wear). Treat everything contacted as contaminated.

**Fit-test every tight-fitting respirator before first use, and again after a change to the face that could affect the seal or a new make or model; fit-check it each time it goes on** — facial hair along the sealing surface stops it sealing properly, and a leaking mask does not give its expected protection. **Record the PPE actually used.**

**Stop and escalate** if a hazard cannot be controlled: licensed electrician, structural engineer, Poisons Information Centre **13 11 26**, or **000** in an emergency.
