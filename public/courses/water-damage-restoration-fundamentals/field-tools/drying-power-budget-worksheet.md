# Drying and power-budget worksheet

**CARSI Water Restoration Practitioner — field tool (Module 5)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian-produced: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO.*

## The four drying levers

1. **Evaporation** — air movers move air across wet surfaces.
2. **Dehumidification** — refrigerant/desiccant dehumidifiers remove the moisture.
3. **Temperature** — warmer air holds and releases moisture more readily (within limits).
4. **Air exchange / containment** — bring in drier air, or seal the zone to control it.

**Rule:** add evaporation and remove moisture **together**. Airflow without dehumidification just raises humidity and stalls drying.

## 230 V / 10 A power budget

One 10 A circuit ≈ **230 V × 10 A = 2300 W**. Outlets often share one breaker. Add machine current draws (from the rating plate, in amps) per circuit and keep each **safely below 10 A** with headroom for start-up surges.

Spread the load across separate circuits; use an RCD; use tested-and-tagged leads only. A tripped breaker **dries nothing overnight**.

## Mould hold-rule (check before placing any air mover)

If visible mould is present: **do not run air movers.** Dry with air filtration and dehumidifiers under **containment** only. Introduce air movers **after remediation and a clearance**. When in doubt, treat suspected mould as mould.
