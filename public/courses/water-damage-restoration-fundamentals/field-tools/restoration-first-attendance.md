# First-attendance roadmap card

**CARSI Water Restoration Practitioner — field tool (Module 1)**
*Australian-produced: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, metric, AUD.*

## The first hour on site — in order

1. **Make it safe.** Stop at the threshold. Look for electrical, structural, biological, slip and chemical hazards before entering. Isolate 230 V power to wet areas at the switchboard; run your own gear through an RCD (safety switch) on tested-and-tagged leads.
2. **Identify the water.** Source, category (1 clean / 2 grey / 3 black) and likely class. When unsure, treat as the worse category and reassess.
3. **Size the loss.** Measure and record the affected floor area in **m²** (length × width per area, then add). Note wall wicking height in **mm**.
4. **Record the air.** Thermo-hygrometer reading on arrival: **°C** and **%RH**. This is the baseline for every later drying decision.
5. **Set expectations.** Explain in plain English what happens next, that drying is measured to a goal, and that a fixed dry time cannot be guaranteed. Pricing is in **AUD** (note GST).

## The restoration arc (reverse)

make safe → understand the water & materials → control the source → assess moisture → dry → monitor to the goal → verify → document

**Remember:** surface dryness is not proof. Build the job file from the first minute.
