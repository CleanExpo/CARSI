## Condensation or leak?

- [ ] Comes and goes with cold nights or thermostat settings
- [ ] Collects on the coldest surfaces first, often as a broad band
- [ ] Present whether or not the heating or cooling runs
- [ ] Spreads from a point, worse after rain or water use

First two ticked: likely condensation. Last two ticked: look for a leak.
