**Electrical, both countries.** Mains is nominally 230 V, 50 Hz.
- **Australia:** portable equipment, power tools and extension leads at a workplace protected by an RCD (WorkSafe ACT; check your state or territory regulator). In-service equipment inspected and tested under AS/NZS 3760:2022.
- **New Zealand:** RCD protection required for plug-in tools in damp conditions (Electricity (Safety) Regulations 2010); WorkSafe says all equipment on construction sites should be RCD-protected. Testing and tagging is not a legal requirement in NZ, but AS/NZS 3760 is one way to show equipment is safe.

## If something goes wrong

- **Australia:** notify the WHS regulator **immediately** of a notifiable incident and preserve the site until an inspector arrives or directs otherwise. Emergency **000**. Poisons Information **13 11 26**.
- **New Zealand:** notify WorkSafe **as soon as possible**, by the fastest means (0800 030 040 for a death). Keep the record for at least five years. Emergency **111**. National Poisons Centre **0800 764 766**.
