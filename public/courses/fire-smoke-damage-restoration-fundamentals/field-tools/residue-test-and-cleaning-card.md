# Residue test and cleaning card

**CARSI Fire & Smoke Restoration Practitioner — field tool (Module 4)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian and New Zealand. 230 V / 50 Hz, metric, AS/NZS. Standards named only (aligned to the principles of ANSI/IICRC S700:2025); no standard text reproduced.*

## Step 1 — identify the residue (CARSI working labels)

| Type | Usual source | Look and feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry | fast, hot fire of natural materials | powdery, smears little |
| Wet | slow, smouldering, low-oxygen fire; plastics and foams | sticky, smeary, strong smell |
| Protein | cooking fires, burnt food | thin yellowish film, very strong odour |
| Fuel-oil | oil heater or equipment fault | oily, dark, travels through ducts |

## Step 3 — method by residue (working guide; the test patch decides)

- **Dry:** HEPA vacuum and dry chemical sponge before any liquid.
- **Wet:** degreasing cleaner suited to the film; ventilation; PPE per the SDS.
- **Protein:** protein-film cleaner; plan for heavy odour work.
- **Fuel-oil:** solvent-based degreaser; check HVAC and ducts.

Read the SDS for every product, in either country.

## Step 4 — sequence

1. Hazardous-materials check cleared for the area.
2. Source removal (charred, melted, saturated materials out; contents photographed and inventoried first).
3. Air filtration running.
4. Top-down: ceiling → walls → fixtures → floor.
5. Least affected rooms first.
6. Wet structure: dry to a measured dry goal. Mould present: contain, filter and dehumidify; no air movers across it until it is cleaned and cleared.
