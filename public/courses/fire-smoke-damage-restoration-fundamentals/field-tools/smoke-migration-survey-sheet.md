## Boundary sketch

Draw the plan on a grid (1 square = 1 m). Mark:
- the affected / unaffected boundary as a heavy line;
- each evidence point with a number that matches the table;
- door gaps, vents and outlets that smoke could pass through.

## Health reminder

Smoke contains PM2.5 particles (under 2.5 microns, or 0.0025 mm) that go deep into the lungs. Wear a fit-tested respirator before you disturb residue, and keep occupants out, especially people with heart or lung conditions, young children and pregnant women.
