# Odour control and job file checklist

**CARSI Fire & Smoke Restoration Practitioner — field tool (Module 6)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian and New Zealand. 230 V / 50 Hz, metric, AS/NZS. Standards named only (aligned to the principles of ANSI/IICRC S700:2025); no standard text reproduced.*

## Odour control — in this order

1. [ ] Source removed (charred and saturated materials out)
2. [ ] Every reachable surface cleaned, including cavities, roof space and HVAC
3. [ ] Porous surfaces that cannot be fully cleaned sealed (product, area, date)
4. [ ] Remaining odour treated with a method matched to the space and occupancy
5. [ ] Result verified and recorded before cavities close or occupants return

## If an ozone treatment is used (consistent with the CARSI Ozone and Hydroxyl Generators course)

- Space **empty**: people and pets out and kept out; every entry signed and secured.
- Plants, rubber items, artwork and susceptible fabrics removed or protected.
- Unit rating plate matches 230 V mains; run from an RCD-protected outlet.
- Worker limit: **0.1 ppm (0.2 mg/m³)** — Australia peak limitation; New Zealand ceiling. It is **not** a clearance level for occupants.
- Re-entry: a site-specific decision from the manufacturer's instructions and calibrated readings.

**Hydroxyl units:** do not assume they are safe with people inside. Ask the supplier for an independent test report (who tested, room, odour source, measured ozone output, by-products measured).

## Verification before handover

- [ ] Surfaces clean to touch and eye
- [ ] Odour assessed room by room by someone fresh to the room
- [ ] Moisture at the dry goal where the building was wet
- [ ] HVAC cleaned, or isolated with referral
- [ ] Asbestos clearance on file where it applied
- [ ] Who checked, when, what they found; failed areas back to cleaning

## Job file index

Site release and make-safe · boundary sketch and survey · hazardous-materials findings and clearances · residue tests · cleaning records · corrosion actions with times · contents inventory · HVAC record · odour steps and readings · moisture readings · scope changes and approvals (who, when) · handover.
