## Rules

1. Compare rooms by **g/kg**, not %RH (RH changes with temperature).
2. Compare **dew point** with **surface temperature**: a surface at or below the dew point condenses.
3. Record what equipment is running. Readings under drying gear on a 230 V circuit are not the building's normal state.
4. Note the calculation method your instrument uses (CARSI worked examples use the Magnus approximation at 101.325 kPa).
