# Assessment report template

**CARSI Indoor Environment Assessment Practitioner — field tool (Module 6)**
*DRAFT for SME review. Australian English, metric units, AUD with GST.*

## Summary (one plain-English paragraph for the occupant)

## 1. Scope and question
Commissioned by · written question · decision supported · exclusions.

## 2. Site
Address · building type · build year · construction · NCC climate zone (AU) or location (NZ) · occupancy.

## 3. Method and instruments
Type · model · serial · field check done today.

## 4. Conditions at the time
Weather · outdoor °C / %RH / g/kg · HVAC and equipment state · doors and windows.

## 5. Observations
What was seen and smelt, with photo references.

## 6. Measurements

| Point | Material / location | Instrument and mode | Value and unit or scale | Reference | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | |

## 7. Laboratory results
Attached as issued · laboratory accreditation (NATA / IANZ) · chain-of-custody form.

## 8. Interpretation
Against references, outdoors and trends · which hypotheses are supported or ruled out, and why.

## 9. Limitations
- [ ] Areas not accessed or opened
- [ ] Measurements not taken, and why
- [ ] Stop events (time, reading, referral)
- [ ] Lost or damaged samples

## 10. Recommendations and referrals
Each tied to a finding, with who should act. Health questions: refer to a doctor.

**Assessor:** name · CARSI designation · date.

---

**Checks before you send**
- [ ] No "safe", "unsafe", "toxic", "clear" or "healthy" without a basis in scope, method and competency.
- [ ] Every regulatory figure source-tagged (jurisdiction, date, units, averaging time) and marked "not a residential threshold".
- [ ] No text pasted from any standard; standards named only where relevant.
- [ ] Same facts in every copy; who commissioned the report is stated.
- [ ] Hand-over package for other professionals: readings, photos, laboratory reports, chain-of-custody forms.
