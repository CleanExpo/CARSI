# Instrument check card

**CARSI Indoor Environment Assessment Practitioner — field tool (Module 3)**
*DRAFT for SME review. Instrument types only; no brand is endorsed.*

| Instrument | Daily field check (per maker's manual) | Record with every reading | Can tell you | Cannot tell you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thermo-hygrometer | Compare with a second instrument; note result | Value, location, height, time, settled | Air °C, %RH; calculated dew point and g/kg | Surface moisture; health risk |
| Moisture meter (pin or non-invasive) | Maker's check; same mode all job | Value, scale, mode, material, point, reference | Relative or material-specific moisture against a reference | A meaningful number without a reference |
| Infrared thermometer / thermal camera | Emissivity setting checked | Surface °C, location, conditions | Surface temperature differences | Moisture (confirm with a meter) |
| CO2 monitor | Outdoor reading at start | ppm, occupancy, HVAC mode, doors and windows | Ventilation for the people present, when no combustion source | Toxicity or a health verdict |
| Particle counter | Zero or purge as the maker sets out | Counts per size channel, sample volume, location | Comparisons with the same instrument | What the particles are |
| ATP luminometer | Maker's control check | Reading, surface, before or after cleaning | Organic residue (general cleanliness) | Mould, a specific germ, sterility |
| Personal CO monitor | Check as the maker sets out | Any alarm: ppm, time, location | When to stop and leave | Whether occupants are safe long-term |

## Power and electrical

- Charge from a **230 V, 10 A GPO** through an **RCD** (Australia: portable equipment and leads at a workplace must be RCD-protected). New Zealand supplies 230 V too.
- Chargers and leads in date under your employer's test-and-tag system.

## A reading you can defend

**Value + unit or scale + instrument and mode + location + time + reference.**
