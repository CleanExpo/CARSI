# Case card IE-001 — Bedroom corner moisture

**CARSI Indoor Environment Assessment Practitioner — case assessment (Module 2)**
*DRAFT for SME review. A fictional CARSI teaching case with illustrative readings. Completing it is
a learning activity only: it does not show competency or give anyone a health clearance.*

## The job

A 1970s weatherboard house in Hobart, Tasmania, in July. Two adults and a five-month-old baby rent
it. There is dark spotting where the main-bedroom ceiling meets the outside wall (south-west
corner), about 0.4 m × 0.3 m. The bedroom is 3.6 m × 3.2 m with a 2.4 m ceiling. Clothes dry on an
airer in the bedroom; the window stays shut at night. A reverse-cycle split system heats the room.
The shower backs onto the bedroom's west wall. A tenant says the gutter "overflowed in the last big
storm". The property manager asks: *What is causing the corner to stay damp, and what should be
done?*

## Your tasks

1. Work out the dew point and g/kg for the bedroom and outdoors (show your working).
2. Fill in three columns: **observation**, **measurement**, **hypothesis**. No causes in the first
   column, no readings in the last.
3. List at least four competing causes and say what supports or weakens each.
4. Write a Five Whys chain, with the evidence for each step, that ends at a cause someone can fix.
5. Say who you refer each finding to (trade, indoor environmental professional, doctor) and what
   you do not do yourself.
6. *New Zealand learners:* relocate the case to a Dunedin rental and say which NZ rental rule
   applies.

Then take the IE-001 quiz (pass mark 80 %).
