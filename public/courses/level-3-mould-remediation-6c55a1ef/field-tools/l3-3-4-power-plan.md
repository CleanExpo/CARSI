# Large-job power plan

CARSI · Level 3 Mould Remediation · Lesson 3.4 · Field download

Australian-produced. Metric units. Use on site, then file it in the job record.

Job address: ______________________   Date: ____/____/______   Supervisor: ______________________

- [ ] Equipment list with the current (A) from each rating plate, at 230 V.
- [ ] Total current and the share on each phase (L1, L2, L3). Phases balanced within a few amps.
- [ ] Licensed electrician engaged for the temporary board or generator connection. Name and licence number.
- [ ] RCD protection on every circuit that feeds equipment. Board inspected and tested before use.
- [ ] Every machine and lead tested and tagged and inside its test date.
- [ ] Leads off wet floors, out of walkways and away from doorways and film walls.
- [ ] Start-up staggered for dehumidifiers.
- [ ] Generator (if used) outdoors, exhaust away from any opening, never in a containment or a roller-door bay.
- [ ] Daily check: tripped RCDs, hot plugs, damaged leads. Faulty gear tagged out.
