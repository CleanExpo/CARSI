# Scales and reliable ranges: field card

**CARSI Professional Moisture Meters — field tool (Module 3)**
*Australian-produced: metric readings throughout.*

## Before you write a reading down

- [ ] Percent only for a calibrated, corrected timber reading, read in the right mode
- [ ] Points for plasterboard, concrete or masonry, and for any reference-scale reading
- [ ] Above 30%: write "above 30% (qualitative)"
- [ ] No decimal places on a qualitative reading
- [ ] Every line carries the scale or mode, the read height (mm) and, for a pin meter, the pin depth (mm)
- [ ] Check the unaffected timber baseline against the room EMC

## How to word a reading

1. Is the meter calibrated for this material? If no: write **points** and the same-material unaffected baseline.
2. Is the reading within about 6% to 30% on timber? If no (above 30%): write **"above 30% (qualitative)"**.
3. If yes to both: write **% MC**, with corrections applied.

| Material | Reading type | Report as | Compare with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Timber, calibrated, corrected, 6%–30% | Quantitative | % MC | Room EMC |
| Timber above 30% | Qualitative | "above 30% (qualitative)" | Re-read daily |
| Plasterboard, concrete, masonry | Qualitative | Points | Same material, meter and scale, unaffected |

Example lines: *Kickboard 21% MC (timber scale, species corrected, 50 mm above slab, pins 4 mm).* / *Plasterboard 64 points versus 12 points unaffected (reference scale, 300 mm).*

## Reliable range on timber

- Resistance (pin) meters: about **6% to 30%**
- Dielectric (pinless) meters: about **4% to 30%**
- The fibre saturation point of wood averages about **30%** moisture content
- Readings above 30% are qualitative only

## Equilibrium moisture content (EMC), Wood Handbook eq. 4-5

| Room | Temperature (°C) | RH (%RH) | EMC (%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical room | 21 | 65 | 12.0 |
| Humid tropical room | 25 | 75 | 14.2 |
| Air-conditioned office | 22 | 45 | 8.4 |

QBCC: timber in houses normally has an in-service moisture content of between 9% and 13%.

## Glossary

- **Quantitative reading:** a material-calibrated reading reported as moisture content.
- **Qualitative reading:** a relative reading, reported in points against an unaffected baseline.
- **Reliable range:** about 6%–30% (resistance) and 4%–30% (dielectric) on timber.
- **EMC:** the moisture content timber settles at in a given air temperature and %RH.
