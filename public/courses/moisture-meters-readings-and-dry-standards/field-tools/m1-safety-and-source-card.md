# Before-you-read safety and source card

**CARSI Professional Moisture Meters — field tool (Module 1)**
*Australian-produced: metric readings throughout.*

## 1. Before the first reading: safety checklist

- [ ] Power to the wet area has been assessed (confirmed with the site supervisor): __________
- [ ] Pins stay well away from GPOs, switches, light fittings and the vertical and horizontal lines where cables and pipes usually run.
- [ ] Pinless scan first on walls; pins only where I have checked what is behind the surface.
- [ ] Hands dry. Meter and charger never tested with wet hands.
- [ ] Batteries charged from an RCD-protected 230 V, 10 A GPO.
- [ ] Contaminated water or visible mould: PPE set by the site safe work method statement worn; electrodes wiped and bagged between areas.
- [ ] Unaffected (control) area tested first.
- [ ] Owner told before pins go into finished surfaces; pin points chosen in inconspicuous places (behind skirting, inside cupboards).
- [ ] New Zealand job: check WorkSafe NZ for the electrical safety position.
