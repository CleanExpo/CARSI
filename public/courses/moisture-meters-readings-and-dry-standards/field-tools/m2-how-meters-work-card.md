# How meters work: field card (one page)

**CARSI Professional Moisture Meters — field tool (Module 2)**
*Australian-produced: metric readings throughout.*

## Formulas and units

| Quantity | Formula | Units |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moisture content (oven-dry basis) | MC = (wet mass − oven-dry mass) ÷ oven-dry mass × 100 | % |
| Average-depth pin setting (insulated pins) | depth ≈ thickness ÷ 5 to thickness ÷ 4 | mm |

Worked check: a 45 mm stud gives 45 ÷ 5 = 9 mm and 45 ÷ 4 = 11.25 mm, so read at about 9 to 11 mm. A 25 mm offcut of 412.6 g wet and 356.0 g oven-dry gives (412.6 − 356.0) ÷ 356.0 × 100 = 15.9%.

## What each instrument measures

| Instrument | Measures | Main limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resistance (pin) meter | Direct-current conductance between two pins | Uninsulated pins report the wettest layer they touch |
| Dielectric (pinless) meter | Dielectric properties of the material at the surface | Surface layers dominate; a dry skin over a wet core can read low |
| Thermo-hygrometer | Air temperature (°C) and relative humidity (%RH) | Reads the air only, not the moisture content of materials |
| Thermal camera | Surface temperature (°C) | A cold patch can be a draught or missing insulation; a meter must confirm |

## Field checklist

- [ ] Record the meter type, mode and scale with every reading
- [ ] Record pin depth and whether pins are insulated
- [ ] Pinless to find the field, pins to confirm at depth
- [ ] Camera finds suspects; meter confirms them
- [ ] Air readings (drying space, unaffected control room, outside) in the same log
- [ ] Oven-dry reference: 101 to 105 °C until the mass stops changing

## Glossary

- **Oven-dry mass:** mass of wood dried at 101 to 105 °C until it stops changing.
- **Resistance (pin) meter:** estimates MC from direct-current conductance between two pins.
- **Dielectric (pinless) meter:** estimates moisture from a surface field; the reading is weighted to the surface layers.
- **Insulated pins:** pins coated except at the tip, so they read only the wood at the tip depth.
