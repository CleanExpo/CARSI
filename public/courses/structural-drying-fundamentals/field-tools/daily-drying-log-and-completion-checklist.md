## Stall diagnosis — change one thing

- Air g/kg not falling → dehumidification short or outside air entering: check depression, seal the zone, add dehumidification. **Not** more air movers.
- Air dry, material static → bound or covered water: add heat, focus airflow, or open it up (asbestos check first).
- Surface below dew point → condensation: lower g/kg or warm the surface.

## If someone is hurt

- New Zealand: notify WorkSafe as soon as possible after a notifiable event; a death is reported immediately on 0800 030 040.
- Australia: follow your state or territory WHS regulator's notification process.
- Poisons: Australia 13 11 26 · New Zealand 0800 764 766. Safety data sheets on site for every chemical used (required in New Zealand under the hazardous-substances rules).
