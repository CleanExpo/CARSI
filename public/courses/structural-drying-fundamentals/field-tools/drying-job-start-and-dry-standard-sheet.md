# Drying job start and dry-standard sheet

**CARSI Structural Drying Practitioner — field tool (Module 1)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian and New Zealand job conditions: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, metric units.*

## 1. Make safe (before any equipment)

- [ ] Power to wet areas isolated; switchboard checked for which circuits feed the wet rooms
- [ ] Water source found and stopped
- [ ] Ceiling linings checked for sagging before anyone works under them
- [ ] Hazards noted: electrical, slip, structural, biological, chemical
- [ ] Duty holder recorded (the business, as PCBU — Australia: WHS laws, or OHS Act 2004 in Victoria; New Zealand: HSWA 2015)

## 2. Asbestos check (before any cutting, lifting or drilling)

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Year built / renovated | |
| Australia: built or renovated before 31 December 2003 (national ban)? Before 1990 = most likely | Yes / No |
| New Zealand: built or renovated before 2000? | Yes / No |
| Suspect materials (textured ceilings, older linings, vinyl, underlay) | |
| Sample taken / results due | |

If yes or unsure: **do not cut.** Treat suspect material as asbestos until results return. You cannot tell by looking. Above 10 m² of non-friable asbestos needs a licensed removalist in both countries; in the ACT, homeowners and tradespeople may not remove any amount.

**Oven-dry check (timber):** water to lose = mass − (mass ÷ (1 + MC now) × (1 + MC reference)).
Example: 1.20 kg at 24 %MC to 11 %MC → 1.20 − 1.074 = 0.126 kg (126 mL).
