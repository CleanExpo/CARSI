# Air-mover placement and material check

**CARSI Structural Drying Practitioner — field tool (Module 3)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Australian and New Zealand job conditions: 230 V / 50 Hz, 10 A GPO, metric units.*

## Hold rules — tick before any air mover is switched on

- [ ] **No visible mould** on or near the wet surfaces. If mould is present: hold air movers; dry with dehumidifiers and air filtration under containment until remediation. Treat suspected mould as mould.
- [ ] **No contaminated (Category 3) debris** in the airflow path. Remove it first.
- [ ] Asbestos check done before skirting is removed or holes are opened (see the Module 1 sheet).

## Placement

- [ ] Airflow on the **wet surfaces**, angled along wet wall bases
- [ ] One direction around the room, each air mover feeding the next
- [ ] Corners, behind doors and under kickboards covered
- [ ] Dehumidification in step with airflow (check room g/kg next visit)
