# Sharps and clinical waste checklist

**CARSI Trauma & Biohazard Decontamination Practitioner — field tool (Module 5)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Check your own state EPA or NZ council rules before the job.*

## 1. Sharps search

- [ ] Good light and a torch; look before you touch
- [ ] Tongs or a probe to move bedding, cushions and clothing
- [ ] Never reach blind into gaps, pockets, bins or bags
- [ ] Carry the container to the sharp; place it on a stable surface
- [ ] Never recap, bend or break a needle
- [ ] Medication found: set aside for the client or police as instructed

Containers: AS/NZS 23907:2023 (replaced AS 4031 and AS/NZS 4261). Confirm the edition your regulator names. **Sharps never go in waste bags, general rubbish or recycling.**
