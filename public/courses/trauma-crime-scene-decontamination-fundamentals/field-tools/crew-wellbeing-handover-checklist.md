# Crew wellbeing and handover checklist

**CARSI Trauma & Biohazard Decontamination Practitioner — field tool (Module 6)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Supervisors notice, ask and connect people to help; they are not counsellors.*

## 1. Before the job

- [ ] Crew told what they are likely to see and smell, without graphic detail
- [ ] Anyone may step back without penalty
- [ ] Clean break space set up away from the affected room

## 2. During the job: rotation and breaks

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Plan by area: total affected m² ÷ m² per block = number of blocks. Example: 14 m² ÷ 3.5 m² = 4 blocks.

## 3. After the job

- [ ] Short debrief: what went well, what was hard, what we change
- [ ] Check-in over the next few days (date: ____ )
- [ ] Support information given

| | Australia | New Zealand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crisis support | Lifeline 13 11 14 · Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636 | Call or text 1737 |
| Emergency | 000 | 111 |

AU: health means physical and psychological health (model WHS Act); traumatic events or materials are a psychosocial hazard; Victoria: Psychological Health Regulations 2025 from 1 December 2025. NZ: HSWA defines health as physical and mental health.

## 4. Verification walk-through (clean PPE, good light)

- [ ] No visible residue in or near the affected area
- [ ] Every porous item marked for removal has gone
- [ ] Full contact time achieved on every disinfected surface
- [ ] No remaining odour source; floor beneath removed coverings treated
- [ ] Personal items in sealed, labelled bags for the family
- [ ] Zone tape and floor protection removed; keys returned

## 5. Job file contents

- [ ] Release and authority record · SWMS and briefing sign-on · fit-test and vaccination confirmations
- [ ] Zone sketch · remove-or-clean decisions with photographs
- [ ] Products, ARTG numbers or NZ labels and SDS, dilutions, areas, contact times
- [ ] Sharps and clinical waste records, contractor docket, tracking or Waste Tracker record
- [ ] Any incident or exposure and the response
- [ ] Written summary for the client
