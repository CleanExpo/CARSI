# Blood and body fluid exposure response card

**CARSI Trauma & Biohazard Decontamination Practitioner — field tool (Module 2)**
*DRAFT v1 for SME review. Not medical advice: get assessed by a doctor the same day.*

## Three routes, one barrier

| Exposure route | Standard-precaution barrier |
| --- | --- |
| Sharps injury (needle, glass, fragment) | Look before you touch; tongs; rigid sharps container |
| Splash to eyes, nose or mouth | Eye protection or face shield; respirator when droplets or dust are made |
| Contact with broken skin | Cover cuts with a waterproof dressing; gloves; hand hygiene after removal |

## If it happens: wash, report, get assessed

1. **Needlestick or cut:** wash with soap and water for **at least 30 seconds**. Do **not** squeeze the wound.
2. **Eyes:** irrigate well with clean water or saline. **Mouth:** spit out and rinse with water.
3. **Clothing:** remove contaminated clothing.
4. **Report** to your supervisor now: time, task, what happened.
5. **Go straight to a doctor or emergency department.** If HIV PEP is recommended it should start as soon as possible and **within 72 hours**.
