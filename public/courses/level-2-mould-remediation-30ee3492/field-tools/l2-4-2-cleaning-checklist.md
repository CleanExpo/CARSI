# HEPA and damp wipe checklist

CARSI · Level 2 Mould Remediation · Lesson 4.2 · Field download

Australian-produced. Metric units. Use on site, then file it in the job record.

Job address: ______________________   Date: ____/____/______   Technician: ______________________

- [ ] HEPA vacuum filter sealed in its housing; bag or canister emptied inside containment.
- [ ] Order: top to bottom, cleanest area first, finishing near the exit.
- [ ] Pass 1: HEPA vacuum all remaining surfaces with a brush attachment, slowly.
- [ ] Pass 2: damp wipe with detergent at the label dilution; one wipe per cloth face, eight faces per cloth.
- [ ] Solution changed after every 1.5 m², or sooner if dirty.
- [ ] Allow surfaces to dry. Pass 3: HEPA vacuum again.
- [ ] Torch held flat to each surface: no visible growth or dust. If any, clean again.
- [ ] Record the areas cleaned in m², the product used and its dilution.
