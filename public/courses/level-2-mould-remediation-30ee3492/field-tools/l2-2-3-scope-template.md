# Level 2 scope template

CARSI · Level 2 Mould Remediation · Lesson 2.3 · Field download

Australian-produced. Metric units. Use on site, then file it in the job record.

Job address: ______________________   Date: ____/____/______   Technician: ______________________

- [ ] Areas: surface, material, length × height, m² (measured or estimated).
- [ ] Containment: type, dimensions in metres, volume in m³, entry and airlock.
- [ ] Air: air scrubber model, rated airflow in m³/h, exhaust point, target air changes per hour.
- [ ] Removal: materials, cut lines in mm or m, fixtures removed or protected.
- [ ] Cleaning: surfaces that stay, method (HEPA then damp wipe), any product with its SDS.
- [ ] Drying: goal per material, dehumidifiers and air movers, expected days.
- [ ] Verification: who verifies, what is checked, how the result is recorded.
- [ ] Waste: bag count, weight estimate in kg, disposal point.
- [ ] Assumptions and exclusions, written plainly.
- [ ] Wetting timeline: when the water started, how long the material stayed wet, and the evidence for both.
- [ ] Reinstatement: separate builder scope. It is building work under the NCC edition in force in that state.
- [ ] Client sign-off: name, date, questions asked and answers given.
