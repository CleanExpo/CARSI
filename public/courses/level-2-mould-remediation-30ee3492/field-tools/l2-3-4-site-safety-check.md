# Daily site safety check

CARSI · Level 2 Mould Remediation · Lesson 3.4 · Field download

Australian-produced. Metric units. Use on site, then file it in the job record.

Job address: ______________________   Date: ____/____/______   Technician: ______________________

- [ ] Every outlet used is RCD protected, or a portable RCD is fitted.
- [ ] All leads and machines tested and tagged; tags in date.
- [ ] Amps per circuit added up from rating plates and below the circuit rating.
- [ ] Leads off wet floors and out of walkways.
- [ ] Work and rest schedule set for the day's temperature; cool water on site.
- [ ] Every chemical labelled, with a current SDS on site. 13 11 26 written on the SDS folder.
- [ ] Cutting fibre cement, tile, grout or masonry? Silica risk assessment done first (required since 1 September 2024). No engineered stone work (banned since 1 July 2024).
- [ ] Exit, first aid kit and nearest hospital known to every worker.
- [ ] Notifiable incident procedure known: notify the regulator, preserve the site.
