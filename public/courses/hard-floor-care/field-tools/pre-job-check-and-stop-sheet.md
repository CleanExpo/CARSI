## 6. Stop rules (all apply before and during the job)

If any rule applies, stop. Record it in the Decision box and tell the person named in section 1.

| No. | Stop rule | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Asbestos cannot be ruled out (no test result or register sighted, a floor laid before the ban date, or any doubt): stop and refer the proposed work for competent, jurisdiction-specific assessment. Do not strip, abrade, machine-treat or remove. This gate also comes before any strip and recoat. Do not resume until a competent person confirms in writing what is permitted. | Asbestos: identify, presume, stop and refer; Resilient floors: strip, rinse, dry and recoat; Quoting: residential and commercial programs; Handover, records and the supervised practical day |
| S2 | Broken, lifting or crumbling tiles: stop, keep people out and report it. | Asbestos: identify, presume, stop and refer |
| S3 | A safety data sheet (SDS) is missing for any product: stop until it is on site and read. | SDS, protective equipment, slip risk and power; Chemistry: soil, surface, label and SDS |
| S4 | Power is unsafe: damaged lead or plug, no RCD, test tag out of date, circuit rating not confirmed, or other loads plus machine currents over the circuit rating. Stop and fix it. | SDS, protective equipment, slip risk and power; Which machine for which job, and the pre-start check |
| S5 | No signs out, no dry walkway, or no barrier where the public can reach: stop and set them up before the first pass. | SDS, protective equipment, slip risk and power; Which machine for which job, and the pre-start check; Handover, records and the supervised practical day |
| S6 | The floor or its coating cannot be identified (including a coating recorded as unknown): stop and ask the maker or an expert. An unknown coating ends the job until advice is obtained. | The floor identification routine; The approved setup record; Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip |
| S7 | The maker's limits or the compatibility cannot be established: stop and obtain technical advice. Do not substitute a generic pressure, pH band, pad colour or speed. | Chemistry: soil, surface, label and SDS; The approved setup record; Tile and grout restorative clean |
| S8 | A stone cannot be identified: treat it as acid-sensitive, use only products the maker states are safe for stone, and obtain advice before anything stronger. | The floor identification routine; Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip |
| S9 | A hidden-spot test shows unexpected change or damage in gloss, colour or surface feel: stop. An approved coating-removal test is meant to lift the coating; record that result, and stop only if the floor under it is changed or damaged. | Chemistry: soil, surface, label and SDS; The approved setup record |
| S10 | The equipment cannot stay within the approved setup limits: stop and change the machine, not the limit. | Which machine for which job, and the pre-start check; The approved setup record |
| S11 | Never mix two products. | SDS, protective equipment, slip risk and power; Chemistry: soil, surface, label and SDS |
| S12 | Grout is loose, crumbling or sandy, or a tile sounds hollow or drummy at the tap-and-scratch test: stop, apply no pressure and quote remedial work or a re-grout. One failure in a high-traffic strip stops and re-quotes that strip. | Tile and grout restorative clean; Quoting: residential and commercial programs |
| S13 | Dull or etched patches on stone: stop and seek professional advice. | Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip |
| S14 | Heavy build-up on stone: refer it to a specialist rather than escalating the chemistry. | Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip |
| S15 | Wax or polish on glazed tile: do not apply it unless the tile maker states it is suitable. | Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip |
| S16 | Never let stripping solution dry on the floor. | Resilient floors: strip, rinse, dry and recoat |
| S17 | Steam on timber: do not use it unless the timber or finish maker approves. | Coated timber floors: damp-only care and referral |
| S18 | A worn or bare timber coating: refer it to a timber floor finisher. Do not coat to hide wear unless the finish maker and a finisher say it is suitable, and do not promise a result that cleaning cannot deliver. | Coated timber floors: damp-only care and referral |
| S19 | Wastewater must never go to the stormwater drain: nothing other than clean rainwater should enter it. | Tile and grout restorative clean; Coatings on tile and stone, sealers and slip; Resilient floors: strip, rinse, dry and recoat; Handover, records and the supervised practical day |
