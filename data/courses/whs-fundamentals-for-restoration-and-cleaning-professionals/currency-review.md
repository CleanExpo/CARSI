## 2. Checked and still current (no change)

Each item has a VERIFIED or JUSTIFIED ledger entry:

- **Governance:** Safe Work Australia is a policy body only; the model laws apply everywhere except Victoria; WA commenced 31 March 2022; Victoria's OHS Act 2004; the regulator names.
- **Duties:** PCBU primary duty (s.19); officer due diligence (s.27); worker duties; industrial manslaughter in every jurisdiction (NSW from 16 September 2024, Tasmania from 2 October 2024, which completed national coverage); Victoria's psychological health regulations from 1 December 2025; the White Card unit CPCWHS1001 (current) and its national recognition.
- **SWMS:** required before high-risk construction work, with 18 categories (r 291 (a)–(r)), including falls over 2 m, asbestos, confined spaces, demolition and energised electrical work.
- **Asbestos:** the 2003 ban; Class A covers all asbestos and Class B non-friable; no licence for 10 m² or less of non-friable asbestos, except in the ACT.
- **Engineered stone:** the ban from 1 July 2024 in every jurisdiction; the import ban from 1 January 2025; legacy work permitted with notification.
- **Crystalline silica (CSS) rules:** apply to materials with at least 1% crystalline silica; controlled processing; a silica risk control plan, air monitoring, health monitoring and training for high-risk processing.
- **Exposure standards:** the respirable crystalline silica (RCS) standard is 0.05 mg/m³ TWA. The WES apply until 30 November 2026 and the WEL from 1 December 2026. Victoria adopts the WEL list on 1 December 2026, with a three-year transition for 15 substances; none is RCS, so the course needs no change.
- **Chemicals:** GHS 7 since 1 January 2023; "SDS" replacing "MSDS"; the SDS 5-year review; the hazardous chemicals register, labelling, placards and manifests; Poisons Information 13 11 26; 000.
- **Respirators:** AS/NZS 1715:2009 and AS/NZS 1716:2012 are still current.
- **Noise:** the exposure standard is LAeq,8h 85 dB(A) and LC,peak 140 dB(C).
- **Electrical:** 230 V / 50 Hz; WHS Reg 164 requires an RCD of 30 mA or less on socket outlets of 20 A or less in hostile or wet environments; construction-site installations must meet AS/NZS 3012 (r 163); AS/NZS 3760 covers testing; portable RCDs are push-button tested daily; use a portable RCD where no fixed one exists.
- **Confined spaces:** permit, atmospheric testing (including H₂S and CO), stand-by person, and rescue procedures (model Code, Nov 2024).
- **Mould guidance:** the enHealth guidance exists (now hosted by the Australian CDC).
- **First aid:** the model Code *First aid in the workplace* (2019).
- **Notifiable incidents:** the three categories; notify immediately; the serious-injury list; keep records 5 years; preserve the site (s.39).
