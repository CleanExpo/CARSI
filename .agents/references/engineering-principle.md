# CARSI engineering principle

**Every feature should make CARSI easier to use, more valuable to restoration professionals, and more commercially successful.**

Skills are specialized workflows. Persistent requirements stay in `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/AGENTS.md`, and `docs/agents/CLAUDE.md`.

Before implementing:

1. State the user/customer outcome and business signal (conversion, completion, engagement, support load).
2. Inspect existing code — do not introduce parallel patterns or duplicate components.
3. Pass the relevant quality gates in `quality-gates.md` for the surfaces you touch.
